Grüezi! I am a biostatistician based in Zurich, Switzerland, working as a Research Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Zurich. My research spans statistical methodology, clinical trials, and meta-research.

I also contribute to the Swiss Reproducibility Network and am a fellow of the Center for Reproducible Science and Research Synthesis at the University of Zurich.

Feel free to get in touch if you have questions or want to collaborate.
